1. Clustering Results Summary

1.1 Final Clustering Solution

We successfully identified 3 distinct store clusters using the ISO_QT_PCA95_KMeans_k3 method:

  • Total Stores Analyzed: 2,557
  • Stores After Outlier Removal: 2,352 (removed 205 outliers, 8%)
  • Final Clusters: 3 clusters with balanced sizes (1.75:1 ratio)
  • Clustering Quality: Silhouette score = 0.586 (exceeds target of ≥ 0.40)
  • Stability: Perfect stability (ARI = 1.0, NMI = 1.0) across bootstrap samples

1.2 Cluster Distribution

import pandas as pd
import numpy as np
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt
import seaborn as sns
from sklearn.cluster import KMeans
from sklearn.preprocessing import QuantileTransformer
from sklearn.decomposition import PCA
from sklearn.ensemble import IsolationForest

# Load and preprocess data
df = pd.read_csv('../data/processed/store_features_enhanced.csv')
FEATURES = [
    'sales_mean', 'total_ad_spend_mean', 'roas_mean', 'conversion_rate_mean', 'website_visits_mean',
    'search_spend_ratio_mean', 'social_spend_ratio_mean',
    'ad_months_active', 'ad_active_ratio', 'spent_any', 'avg_ad_spend_active_only'
]

X = df[FEATURES].fillna(0)

# Preprocessing pipeline
iso = IsolationForest(contamination=0.08, random_state=42)
inlier_mask = iso.fit_predict(X) == 1
X_clean = X[inlier_mask]
qt = QuantileTransformer(output_distribution='normal', random_state=42)
X_qt = qt.fit_transform(X_clean)
pca = PCA(n_components=0.95, random_state=42)
X_pca = pca.fit_transform(X_qt)

# KMeans clustering
kmeans = KMeans(n_clusters=3, random_state=42, n_init=100, max_iter=1000)
labels = kmeans.fit_predict(X_pca)

# Map labels back to original store IDs
df_clean = df[inlier_mask].copy()
df_clean['cluster'] = labels

# Cluster distribution
cluster_counts = pd.Series(labels).value_counts().sort_index()
cluster_pct = cluster_counts / len(df_clean) * 100

print("=== Cluster Distribution ===")
for cluster_id in sorted(df_clean['cluster'].unique()):
    count = cluster_counts[cluster_id]
    pct = cluster_pct[cluster_id]
    print(f"Cluster {cluster_id}: {count} stores ({pct:.1f}%)")

print(f"\nTotal stores: {len(df_clean)}")
print(f"Balance ratio: {cluster_counts.max() / cluster_counts.min():.2f}:1")

import numpy as np
from sklearn.cluster import KMeans

2.2 Cluster 0: Low-Volume, High-ROAS Stores

Characteristics: - Size: 621 stores (26.4% of total) - Average Sales: $14.79 per month (lowest) - Average Ad Spend: $502.90 per month (lowest) - ROAS: 0.360 (highest among all clusters) - Ad Active Ratio: 0.38 (lowest, 38% of months with ad activity) - Channel Preference: Social media focused (22% social, 0% search) - Avg Ad Spend (Active Months): $1,175.86

Key Insights: - Small-scale stores with efficient ad spending - Highest ROAS indicates effective use of limited ad budget - Prefer social media advertising over search - Low ad activity frequency but higher efficiency when active

2.3 Cluster 1: High-Volume, Low-ROAS Stores

Characteristics: - Size: 1,085 stores (46.1% of total, largest cluster) - Average Sales: $35.25 per month (highest) - Average Ad Spend: $6,114.21 per month (highest) - ROAS: 0.027 (lowest among all clusters) - Ad Active Ratio: 0.67 (highest, 67% of months with ad activity) - Channel Preference: Search focused (34% search, 12% social) - Avg Ad Spend (Active Months): $9,171.31

Key Insights: - Large-scale stores with high sales volume - Highest ad spending but lowest ROAS efficiency - Most active in advertising (67% of months) - Prefer search advertising over social media - High absolute sales but inefficient ad spending

Characteristics: - Size: 646 stores (27.5% of total) - Average Sales: $21.70 per month (medium) - Average Ad Spend: $2,052.97 per month (medium) - ROAS: 0.317 (medium, second highest) - Ad Active Ratio: 0.51 (medium, 51% of months with ad activity) - Channel Preference: Balanced (21% search, 14% social) - Avg Ad Spend (Active Months): $3,813.74

Key Insights: - Medium-scale stores with balanced performance - Moderate ad spending with reasonable ROAS - Balanced channel strategy (both search and social) - Moderate ad activity frequency - Most balanced cluster in terms of metrics

3. Business Recommendations

3.1 Recommendations for Cluster 0: Low-Volume, High-ROAS Stores

Strengths: - Highest ROAS efficiency (0.360) - Effective use of limited ad budget - Strong social media presence

Recommendations:

  1. Maintain Current Strategy:
    • Continue focusing on social media advertising
    • Keep ad spending low but consistent
    • Monitor ROAS closely to maintain efficiency
  2. Scale Up Carefully:
    • Gradually increase ad spending to test if ROAS can be maintained at higher volumes
    • Test additional social media channels
    • Consider expanding to display/video advertising if social media shows continued success
  3. Optimize Social Media Campaigns:
    • Focus on content quality and engagement
    • Test different social media platforms
    • Leverage user-generated content and influencer partnerships
  4. Improve Ad Activity Consistency:
    • Increase ad activity ratio from 38% to 50%+
    • Develop a consistent monthly advertising schedule
    • Create a content calendar for social media posts
  5. Customer Retention:
    • Focus on customer loyalty programs
    • Implement email marketing campaigns
    • Leverage social media for customer engagement

3.2 Recommendations for Cluster 1: High-Volume, Low-ROAS Stores

Strengths: - Highest sales volume ($35.25/month) - High ad activity (67% of months) - Strong search advertising presence

Recommendations:

  1. Improve ROAS Efficiency (Priority):
    • Reduce ad spending by 20-30% to test if sales can be maintained
    • Focus on high-performing keywords and campaigns
    • Eliminate low-performing ad campaigns
    • Improve ad targeting and bidding strategies
  2. Optimize Search Advertising:
    • Conduct keyword research to identify high-converting keywords
    • Improve ad copy and landing pages
    • Test different ad formats (text, display, shopping ads)
    • Implement conversion tracking and attribution modeling
  3. Diversify Channel Mix:
    • Reduce reliance on search advertising (currently 34%)
    • Test social media advertising (currently only 12%)
    • Consider display/video advertising
    • Implement multi-channel attribution
  4. Improve Conversion Rate:
    • Optimize website user experience
    • Improve landing page quality and relevance
    • Test different pricing strategies
    • Implement A/B testing for key pages
  5. Ad Activity Optimization:
    • While ad activity is high (67%), focus on quality over quantity
    • Pause underperforming campaigns
    • Focus budget on high-performing periods
    • Implement seasonal ad scheduling

3.3 Recommendations for Cluster 2: Medium-Volume, Balanced Stores

Strengths: - Balanced performance across metrics - Good ROAS (0.317, second highest) - Balanced channel strategy

Recommendations:

  1. Maintain Balanced Strategy:
    • Continue balanced approach to advertising
    • Maintain current channel mix (21% search, 14% social)
    • Keep ad spending at moderate levels
  2. Gradual Growth:
    • Increase ad spending by 10-15% to test growth potential
    • Test new channels (display, video) while maintaining balance
    • Monitor ROAS to ensure efficiency is maintained
  3. Optimize Channel Performance:
    • Identify best-performing channels and allocate more budget
    • Test different ad formats within each channel
    • Implement cross-channel attribution
  4. Improve Ad Activity Consistency:
    • Increase ad activity ratio from 51% to 60%+
    • Develop a consistent advertising schedule
    • Create a content calendar for all channels
  5. Customer Acquisition and Retention:
    • Focus on both customer acquisition and retention
    • Implement loyalty programs
    • Leverage email marketing
    • Test different customer segments

5. Key Findings and Insights

5.1 Summary of Key Findings

  1. Three Distinct Store Segments: Successfully identified 3 distinct store clusters with different characteristics and needs

  2. ROAS Efficiency Trade-off: Higher sales volume does not necessarily mean higher ROAS efficiency

    • Cluster 0: Low volume, high ROAS (0.360)
    • Cluster 1: High volume, low ROAS (0.027)
    • Cluster 2: Medium volume, medium ROAS (0.317)

  3. Channel Preferences: Different clusters prefer different advertising channels

    • Cluster 0: Social media focused (22%)
    • Cluster 1: Search focused (34%)
    • Cluster 2: Balanced (21% search, 14% social)

  4. Ad Activity Patterns: Ad activity frequency varies by cluster

    • Cluster 0: 38% (lowest)
    • Cluster 1: 67% (highest)
    • Cluster 2: 51% (medium)

  5. Size Distribution: Cluster 1 is the largest (46.1%), indicating most stores are high-volume, low-ROAS stores

  • Balanced Strategy: Cluster 2 can maintain balance while growing

We successfully identified 3 distinct store clusters with different characteristics, performance levels, and advertising strategies. Each cluster requires customized recommendations to optimize performance:
